Track: Wearables, plus Gemini, ElevenLabs, Tiger Data, GoDaddy domain, Bambu 3D print and Vultr.
Hardware: Raspberry Pi 4B as the brain, with the Grove 3-axis accelerometer, Grove Servo tappers for haptics and a USB-C power bank. No moving weights.
Prints: Bambu enclosures for the Pi, the servo tappers and the sensor mount.
Data path: the Pi posts to Next.js, which runs a custom WebSocket server on a Vultr VM and writes to Tiger Data.
App: an Expo Go iOS app that gets WebSocket events and plays ElevenLabs spoken corrections.
Reports: Gemini clinician reports, generated after each session and on demand.
Tiger Data: all 4 ideas, focused on ingestion and anomaly windows.
Team: 4 people, one owner per area.
